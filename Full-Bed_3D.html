Full-Bed · Master Assembly · V1▾

Canonical full-bed master assembly: detailed open-back headboard, the 74 x 85 grounded 6-drawer base, and the 74 x 41 x 16 TV-lift footboard. V1 builder-review geometry; sample/builder-confirm items are labeled.

The headboard remains the detailed 130 x 72 x 8 in open-back assembly. Front face is twelve 7/8 in removable flush front skins (four horizontal courses x three bays, seams at AFF 20/40/60); structural loads land in the side stiles, two full-depth mullions, deeper receiver rails, and the lower receiver ladder.

The top mullions use the current 3-land W-relief: three 1.5 in lands (front / center / rear) with two 1.75 in gaps for the 35 mm channels, 4.0 in service drop. The cap pulls down with three vertical insert draw-bolts per mullion (one per land).
