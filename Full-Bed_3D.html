Canonical full-bed master assembly: detailed ventilated-back headboard (3 bolt-together boxes; 3 butted top-cap segments), the 74 x 85 grounded 6-drawer base, and the 74 x 41 x 16 TV-lift footboard. Builder-review geometry; sample/builder-confirm items are labeled.

The headboard is the detailed 130 x 72 x 8 in assembly with a VENTILATED plywood back (sealed-but-vented + serviceable, D0-2: bottom cool-air intake + top hot-air exhaust past the LED-channel mount gap, removable ~30 in lower center service panel; freestanding, no wall cleat). Front face is twelve 7/8 in recessed field skins (four horizontal courses x three bays, seams at AFF 19/38/57, recessed 3/16 in behind the proud face-frame; standard courses glued+screwed, the speaker course screw-only); structural loads land in the built-up L side stiles, two hollow built-up mullions, three per-box built-up L bottom rails, and the AFF 19/38/57 course cross-rails.

The top mullions use the current 3-land W-relief: three 1.5 in lands (front / center / rear) with two 1.75 in gaps for the 35 mm channels, 4.0 in service drop. The cap pulls down with three vertical insert draw-bolts per mullion (one per land).
