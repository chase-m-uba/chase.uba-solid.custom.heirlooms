Design rules
  • Front skins are captured on all four edges by ash retainer strips bolted to the frame; one datum + slight clearance per skin so each course can move seasonally without splitting.
  • No through-holes in the headboard front skins — USB-C and wiring enter from the open rear cavity only.
  • All wide panels are frame-and-panel with floating insets; keep unbroken cross-grain spans under ~24 in.
  • The top cap floats on its joint (not glued cross-grain) to allow seasonal wood movement.
  • The headboard back stays open — no continuous rear panel.
Design decisions
  • Front-skin retention upgraded from small clips to a 4-sided beefy ash retainer (per the builder's reference build).
  • TV-cabinet-to-base seam is bolts-only: four bed bolts plus one grommeted wire pass-through. (4 bolts)
  • Nightstands cantilever from the headboard rear (no legs to the floor).
  • Delivered raw / unfinished — the owner applies the finish (black ink dye + matte topcoat).
Facts & notes
  • Large solid-wood panels crack across the grain — every wide face is frame-and-panel with floating insets.
  • Quartersawn stock is preferred for the visible faces (about 2× the wood-movement stability of flatsawn).
  • The bed ships flat and panelized for upstairs delivery (sections about 90 in or under).
